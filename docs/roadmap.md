# Playable delivery roadmap

The [GDD](design/ProjectQuest_Comprehensive_Game_Design_Document.docx) targets a full career simulation across six sectors, but its vertical-slice gate is one deep construction internship mission. The implementation sequence below keeps each milestone runnable and separately verifiable. A completed milestone is tested, documented, committed, pushed to the canonical GitHub repository, and its remote commit checked before the next milestone is completed.

## Current implementation status

The browser foundation and construction Day 1 were delivered on `feat/projectquest-browser`. The subsequent sound, typography and committed-choice milestone is pushed as `6018a38`: original locally bundled music/effects, self-hosted licensed fonts, persisted audio controls and journal-backed response locks.

Version 0.2.0 adds the playable Day 2 chapter, **The Slipping Milestone** (`CONST-I02`), with an editable four-task schedule, two successor branches, conditional recovery approval, carried project consequences and saved career case studies. Current validation and remote delivery are recorded in [verification.md](verification.md); no test count or push status for this increment is inferred from an earlier run.

Only construction is playable. Day 3, **The Stakeholder Check-in**, and Days 4–5 remain planned. The development branch remains separate from `main`; no merge or deployment is claimed. Human learner/SME validation, representative-device benchmarks, broader accessibility evaluation and the full career remain future work.

## Status discipline

The repository's checks and commit history are the evidence of delivered engineering. [testing.md](testing.md) describes checks; [verification.md](verification.md) records concrete results and delivery blockers. A future industry card, roadmap item, data contract, or test plan does not imply playable content. Outstanding bespoke character/environment art, richer animation/audio, representative-device benchmarks, learner research and sector review remain visible production work even after the initial slice passes engineering checks.
